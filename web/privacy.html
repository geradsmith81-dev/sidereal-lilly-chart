Privacy policy

The Sidereal Oracle (website siderealoracle.com and the Android app com.siderealoracle.app)
Effective 24 November 2026

In short

The Sidereal Oracle has no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no trackers. It does not keep a database of users. What you type is used only to draw and read your chart, and it is never sold or shared.

What you may enter

  • A date, time and place for a chart, and optionally the elevation of the place.
  • Optionally, your device’s position, if you press Use my location.
  • Optionally, a profile: your name, a picture, your birth date, birth time and birthplace, and the place where you live.
  • Optionally, friends: the same details for people who have sent you their friend card.

All of this is optional except the date, time and place of the chart you ask for. Please add a friend only with their agreement: friend cards are meant to be swapped, not copied.

How it is used

The charts are calculated on the app’s server from NASA JPL planet tables. Your entries are therefore sent to the server over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, used to calculate and display the chart, and kept in the server’s working memory only while your app is open. They are not written to the server’s disk, not put in a database, and not used for anything else. A picture you choose is shrunk to a small image on the server and sent back to your device.

To make repeat views faster, a calculated chart (a time and a place, with no name attached) may stay in the server’s memory for up to one hour. Restarting the server clears it.

Your location and ground height

Use my location (in your profile, under Where you live) asks your phone or computer for its position. The profile window asks once by itself when no home place is set yet, and again whenever you press the button. Your browser asks your permission first, and you can always type a town instead. The position is rounded to about 10 metres and used only to fill in where you live; the nearest town gives its name and time zone. The app never tracks you and never reads your position in the background.

To set the ground height (elevation) of a place you choose, the server looks it up in public elevation maps: the Terrain Tiles of the AWS Registry of Open Data, and over water or when those cannot be reached, the Open-Meteo Elevation API. Only the place’s coordinates (or the map tile that contains them) are sent, from the app’s server, never with your name or birth details.

What stays on your device

  • If you tick Remember me on this device, your profile and friends are saved in this browser’s or app’s own storage on your phone or computer, not on the server. Forget me on this device deletes that copy; uninstalling the app or clearing the browser’s site data does too.
  • If you do not tick it, your profile is forgotten when you close the app.
  • Friend cards (.lillycard files, text codes and QR codes) are made by the app while it is open, are not kept on the server, and travel only where you send them. A QR code or link contains the card itself, so anyone you give it to can read the birth details in it.
  • Calendar files (.ics) and forecast downloads are saved only where you save them.

Cookies and technical records

The app uses one technical security cookie that the Streamlit software needs to work. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

The app is hosted by Render (render.com), on servers in the United States. Like any web host, Render may keep short-lived technical records of connections, such as the internet (IP) address, the time and the web address requested, for security and to keep the service running. If you open a friend-card link, the card is part of that web address. These records are covered by Render’s own privacy policy; The Sidereal Oracle does not use them to identify or follow anyone.

Sharing

Nothing you enter is sold, rented or shared with anyone, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI systems. The readings come from fixed rules taken from Lilly’s 1647 book: no AI chatbot writes them.

Links to other sites

Page references link to the scanned book on the Internet Archive (archive.org). Opening such a link takes you to that site, which has its own privacy policy.

Children

The Sidereal Oracle is meant for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your information

As nothing about you is stored on the server, there is nothing to delete there. To remove what is stored on your device, use Forget me on this device, clear the site’s data, or uninstall the app.
